The funniest one-line famous last words, a tombstone epitaph of 150 characters or fewer — an entry for make.fun's Famous Last Words: The Funniest Epitaph bounty ($2, 1st $1 / 2nd $1). Round 2 entry: a genuinely different epitaph from round 1 (round 1 was about cancelling subscriptions; this round is about the update nag that finally got him).

## Entry

> Here lies Dave. He pressed 'Remind me later' one too many times.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
